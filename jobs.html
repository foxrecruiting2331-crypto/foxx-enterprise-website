Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about finding your next driving job? We’re here to help!

How do I apply for a truck driving job?

Browse our available positions and click Apply Now. You can also get matched with a recruiter who can help you explore your options.

How much CDL experience do I need?

Requirements vary by position. Some jobs require 3 months of experience, while others require 6 months, 1 year, or more. Check each listing for details.

What types of trucking jobs are available?

Available opportunities may include local, dedicated, regional, intermodal, and over-the-road positions.

Can I find a job that gets me home daily?

Yes! Use the Home Time filter to look for home-daily, home-weekly, and home-weekends opportunities.

How much can I earn?

Pay varies by carrier, route, experience, and pay structure. Review each listing and confirm current pay details with your recruiter.

Do you have no-touch freight jobs?

Yes, no-touch freight opportunities may be available. Use the Freight Type filter to narrow your search.

What if there are no jobs near me?

Try searching by job title or location, or contact us to get matched with a recruiter who can help you explore available positions.

How do I speak with a recruiter?

Click the Get Matched With a Recruiter button and submit the form so our team can help you find a suitable opportunity.

Not Sure Which Job Fits You?
