Welcome to Foxx Enterprise

More Than Recruiting. Building Relationships. Changing Lives.

My name is Allison Fox, CEO of Foxx Enterprise. I founded this company with a vision that goes beyond simply helping truck drivers find jobs. I believe every driver deserves to be treated with respect, honesty, and appreciation. Behind every CDL is a person, a family, and a future that matters.

At Foxx Enterprise, we understand that finding the right driving position is about more than a paycheck. It's about finding the right company, the right opportunity, a schedule that works for your life, and a place where you feel valued.

We work with more than 50 carriers, giving us the ability to connect drivers with opportunities that fit their experience, qualifications, goals, and home-time needs. Whether you're looking for local, regional, dedicated, or OTR opportunities, our goal is to help you find the right seat.

What We Stand For

People Over Profits. We don't believe drivers should ever feel like just another number or dollar sign. We believe in putting people first.

Honesty & Transparency. We believe in being upfront about opportunities, expectations, pay, and home time so drivers can make informed decisions.

Relationships That Matter. We aren't here just to fill a truck. We are here to build relationships, earn trust, and be someone drivers can count on.

Finding the Right Fit. Every driver has different needs. We take the time to understand what matters to you and work to match you with an opportunity that makes sense for your life.

Commitment & Support. Our job doesn't end with an application. We believe in communication, follow-through, and helping drivers every step of the way.

Our Promise to You

At Foxx Enterprise, you are more than a CDL, an application, or a paycheck. You are a person with goals, responsibilities, and a family who depends on you.

We want to help you move forward, find better opportunities, and make decisions that are right for you.

We are proud of what we do, but even more proud of the people we get to do it for.

We care about you, not just the money.
