About Foxx Enterprise

At Foxx Enterprise, we believe truck drivers deserve more than just a job — they deserve an opportunity that fits their lifestyle, goals, and family needs.

We specialize in connecting truck drivers with trucking opportunities that match their experience, qualifications, preferred home time, and pay expectations. We work with over 50 carriers, giving drivers access to a variety of local, regional, and over-the-road positions.

Our mission is simple: help drivers find the right seat without treating them like just another number. We take the time to understand what matters most to each driver and help them explore opportunities that work best for them.

We also welcome motivated individuals who want to become part of our recruiting team. Our recruiter opportunities offer commission-based earning potential, training, resources, and support to help recruiters connect drivers with available positions.

At Foxx Enterprise, we believe in building relationships, creating opportunities, and putting people first.
